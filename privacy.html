Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Urban Store Watches, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you visit or use our website, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when using our website, including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, where provided
  • Billing and shipping information, where applicable
  • Order and transaction information
  • Customer-support enquiries
  • Information submitted through contact forms

We may also automatically collect limited technical information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Referring website
  • Website interaction and usage information
  • Approximate location derived from technical information

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process and manage orders
  • Provide customer support
  • Respond to enquiries
  • Communicate regarding orders, shipping, returns, or refunds
  • Improve our website and services
  • Understand website usage and performance
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity
  • Maintain website security
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

We only use personal information for appropriate business purposes and as permitted by applicable law.

3. Payment Information

Where online payments are available, payment transactions may be handled by third-party payment processors.

Urban Store Watches does not need to store your complete credit or debit card information on its own website servers.

Payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep the website functioning properly
  • Remember preferences
  • Improve website performance
  • Understand visitor traffic
  • Analyze website usage
  • Support security and fraud prevention

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function correctly if certain cookies are disabled.

5. Information Sharing

We may share personal information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our website and provide services.

These providers may include:

  • Payment processors
  • Shipping and delivery providers
  • Website hosting providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Customer-support services
  • Security and fraud-prevention providers
  • Technology and infrastructure providers

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable contractual or legal requirements.

6. Shipping and Delivery Information

When you place an order, we may share necessary information, such as your name, shipping address, email address, phone number, and order details, with shipping or delivery providers so that your order can be fulfilled.

Only information reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service should be shared.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

However, no online service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

This may include retaining information to:

  • Complete transactions
  • Provide customer service
  • Handle returns and refunds
  • Resolve disputes
  • Maintain business records
  • Meet tax, accounting, legal, or regulatory obligations
  • Prevent fraud or misuse

When information is no longer required, we may securely delete or anonymize it where appropriate.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Object to certain processing activities
  • Request a copy of certain personal information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise an applicable privacy right, please contact us using the contact information provided on our website.

10. United States Customers

Privacy rights may vary depending on the state in which you reside.

Where applicable, Urban Store Watches will handle privacy requests in accordance with relevant state privacy laws.

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration.

11. United Kingdom Customers

For customers in the United Kingdom, we aim to process personal information in accordance with applicable UK data-protection requirements.

Where required, we will provide information regarding the legal basis for processing personal information and applicable individual rights.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience and is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

13. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, services, or platforms.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website before providing personal information.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to:

  • Our website
  • Products and services
  • Technology
  • Data-processing practices
  • Legal or regulatory requirements

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised Last Updated date.
