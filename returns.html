Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Urban Store Watches, we want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If you are not completely satisfied with your watch, you may be eligible to return it in accordance with the conditions below.

1. Return Window

We accept eligible returns within 30 days of delivery.

To qualify for a return, the watch must:

  • Be unused and unworn
  • Be in its original condition
  • Include the original packaging
  • Include any tags, manuals, warranty cards, or accessories supplied with the product
  • Not show signs of damage, scratches, or alteration

Items returned after the 30-day return period may not be accepted.

2. Watch-Specific Return Conditions

Returned watches must be in new and resalable condition.

A return may be rejected if the watch has:

  • Visible scratches or dents
  • Signs of wear
  • Damage to the case, dial, crystal, strap, or bracelet
  • Missing links or accessories
  • A modified or resized bracelet
  • Been engraved or otherwise personalized
  • Been repaired or altered by an unauthorized person
  • Missing original packaging or supplied accessories

If you need to adjust a metal bracelet, we recommend confirming the correct fit before making any permanent alteration.

3. Personalized or Customized Watches

Personalized, engraved, or specially modified watches may be non-returnable, except where the item is defective, damaged, incorrectly made, or applicable law provides otherwise.

Any applicable restrictions will be communicated before purchase.

4. How to Request a Return

Before sending an item back, please contact us at:

Email: hello@urbanstore.com

Please include:

  • Order number
  • Name used for the order
  • Watch you wish to return
  • Reason for the return
  • Photographs, if the watch is damaged or defective

We will provide return instructions after reviewing your request.

Please do not send a return to an address without receiving return instructions from us.

5. Return Shipping

For a standard change-of-mind return, the customer may be responsible for the cost of return shipping unless otherwise stated at checkout or required by applicable law.

If a watch is:

  • Defective
  • Damaged during delivery
  • Incorrectly shipped

we will review the issue and may provide an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement, repair, exchange, or refund.

6. Damaged or Incorrect Watches

Please contact us as soon as possible if your package arrives damaged or you receive the wrong watch.

We may request photographs of:

  • The watch
  • The product packaging
  • The shipping package
  • The shipping label

This information helps us investigate the issue.

7. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect an eligible returned watch, we will notify you whether the return has been approved.

If approved, the refund will generally be issued to the original payment method.

Refund processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

Original shipping charges may not be refundable unless the return is due to an error, defect, damage, or another circumstance where a refund is required by applicable law.

8. Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an eligible watch for another product, please contact us before returning the item.

Exchanges are subject to product availability.

If the requested replacement is unavailable, we may offer a refund or another suitable option.

9. Warranty

If a watch is sold with a manufacturer's warranty or Urban Store warranty, warranty coverage will be subject to the applicable warranty terms.

Unless otherwise stated, warranty coverage may not include:

  • Normal wear and tear
  • Scratches or cosmetic damage
  • Accidental damage
  • Damage caused by improper use
  • Unauthorized repairs or modifications
  • Damage caused by water where water resistance was not specified

This does not affect any mandatory warranty or consumer rights provided by applicable law.

10. Water Damage

Customers should follow the water-resistance information provided for their specific watch.

If a product is not specifically described as water-resistant, it should be kept away from water and excessive moisture.

Water-related damage caused by use outside the stated product specifications may not qualify for a return, refund, or warranty claim, subject to applicable law.

11. Lost or Undelivered Returns

Customers are responsible for ensuring that returned watches are properly packaged and shipped to the address provided by Urban Store Watches.

Because watches can be valuable items, we recommend using a tracked shipping service and retaining proof of postage.

We may not be able to process a refund for a return that does not reach us.

12. Sale and Promotional Items

Sale or promotional watches may be subject to different return conditions.

Any special return restrictions will be clearly communicated on the relevant product page or at checkout.

Nothing in this section limits any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

13. Consumer Rights

This policy does not affect any mandatory consumer rights available under applicable law.

Customers in the United States, United Kingdom, and other jurisdictions may have statutory rights regarding defective products, cancellations, refunds, warranties, and other consumer protections.

Where applicable law provides greater rights than this policy, the applicable law will take precedence.

14. Changes to This Policy

Urban Store Watches may update this Return & Refund Policy from time to time.

The latest version will be posted on this page with the updated Last Updated date.

15. Contact Us

For return, exchange, warranty, or refund questions, please contact:
